Parents, before you choose

Questions worth asking.

What safety systems are in place?

KSAR's safety-first approach includes CCTV monitoring, controlled access, trained/verified caregivers, hygiene practices, first-aid preparedness and fire-safety procedures.

Can I visit the center before admission?

Yes. The experience is designed around a personal visit so parents can see the environment and meet the team.

What age groups and timings are offered?

Final age groups, timings and service packages will be added after the center confirms its operating details.

How will parents stay connected?

The final parent communication process can include daily updates, attendance, meals, naps, activities, moments and other features shown in the parent-experience concept.
